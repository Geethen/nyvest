4. Findings and next steps

Support-size sweep: gains continue past 15k

  • tabicl_zero_20k is the new best: F1 = 0.6871, σ = 0.0144. Fold variance collapses as context grows — fold 2 jumps from 0.646 (10k) to 0.678 (20k). This is the most reliable configuration tested to date.
  • The gain per added 5k rows: +0.002 (5k→10k), +0.011 (10k→15k), +0.007 (15k→20k). The curve is decelerating but hasn't flattened. 25k is the natural next step — fold 2 has ~50k augmented rows so it's feasible.

KV cache: free 1.5–1.9× speedup, zero F1 cost

  • F1 is identical to 4 decimal places with kv_cache=True vs False across all support sizes.
  • Prediction time: 6.3s → 4.1s (10k), 7.3s → 4.4s (15k), 8.7s → 4.6s (20k).
  • Fit time increases (1.2s → 3.1s) because the KV precomputation happens during fit. Net is still a win for any test set > ~5k rows.
  • Practical unlock: with kv_cache=True, n_estimators=16 at 20k support costs ~9s total vs ~8.7s for n_est=8 without cache. Test this next.

Ensemble: blocked by CatBoost's weak fold

  • ensemble_avg = 0.6717 — sits between CB_hi (0.6582) and TabICL_10k (0.6728). Averaging with the weaker CatBoost drags TabICL down on fold 2.
  • Arithmetic mean = geometric mean here; no benefit from log-space blending.
  • A more useful ensemble: TabICL_20k (seed 0) + TabICL_20k (seed 1). Two independent random support samples; both strong. Would cost ~9s × 2 = 18s prediction.

Fine-tuning: consistent stability advantage, dominated on mean

  • tabicl_ft_30ep: F1 = 0.6559, σ = 0.004 — tightest fold variance of all. Useful for production deployments where worst-fold performance is the KPI.
  • tabicl_ft_strat collapses (F1 = 0.6157) — confirmed again that stratified support hurts fine-tuning. Random support only.

Per-class highlights

  • Wetland (7): 0.584 → 0.653 with TabICL_20k (+0.069). Crosses 0.65.
  • Scrub (6): 0.626 → 0.664 with TabICL_20k (+0.038). Crosses 0.65.
  • Grassland (5): 0.364 → 0.415 (+0.051). Best so far but ceiling unclear. Feature overlap with cropland (class 3) is the binding constraint. A biased support set (more class-5 rows) may help without oversampling.
  • Bare (merged 1+2): best = 0.511 (TabICL_20k). Below the original class-2-only baseline of 0.548. The merge is ecologically cleaner but spectrally harder.
