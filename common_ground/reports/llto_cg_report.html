Key caveats

  • Fold imbalance. KMeans yields geographically compact, not equal-sized, folds. Fold 1 has only 369 unstable training rows — Stage 2 there is essentially the stable-only baseline.
  • Approximate exchangeability. RAPS is calibrated on stable pixels (year=2020); applied to unstable pixels across 2017–2025. The conformal coverage guarantee is exact on stable, heuristic on unstable.
  • Single test year. Test grading uses year=2020 only (the stable parquet). Multi-year naive vs CG comparison awaits the all-years stable extraction.
  • No filter on t₀. Stage 1 trains on all stable_train rows; trusted labels are not subject to conformal filtering.
