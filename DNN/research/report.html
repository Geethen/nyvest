Why paired per-fold deltas

Fold spread (~0.02) dwarfs the effects we chase (~0.002), so comparing run means mostly compares fold noise. The folds are fixed and deterministic, so each run is scored against the baseline fold by fold.

What counts as a win

A result must improve every fold and clear +0.003 mean — roughly one seed-noise sigma. Anything inside ±0.003 is recorded as a tie, not a gain.

What prior work established

Capacity is not the lever: residual nets hit 0.965 validation while test stayed at 0.71. The hard vegetation classes are confusion-bound — learning curves flatten by 70% of the data.

The oracle bound closes the question

On fold 0 the 15-member ensemble is 78.7% accurate. If an oracle picked the right member for every row it would reach 85.1% — +6.3 points of headroom that no routing scheme can reach. Members disagree on just 5.9% of rows, and their disagreement does not track correctness.

Diversity contributes nothing

Fifteen architecturally diverse members (MLP + SwiGLU + RMSNorm + SAM + EMA) score 0.7322; fifteen identical-architecture seeds score 0.7321. Same size, same budget — a 0.0001 difference. Only member count ever mattered, and only up to five.

The ceiling is information, not modelling

Validation ranges from 0.72 to 0.94 across these runs; test never leaves 0.70–0.73. Everything added gets absorbed into fitting the training regions and none of it crosses the fold boundary.

Both directions lose

IRM removed region-specific features and lost 0.024. Neighbour features added them and lost 0.029. Those features are not shortcuts — they are the signal, which is why held-out geography stays hard.

Why lidar was different

Elevation and canopy height describe what is physically present, and physics holds across Norway. Neighbour statistics describe what is nearby here. Only transferable physical measurements have ever moved this number.
